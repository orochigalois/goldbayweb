01

Fund of FundFOF 基金中基金

Diversified, manager-agnostic, globally allocated.多元分散，管理人中立，全球配置。

By carefully selecting excellent fund managers around the world and their differentiated strategy products, we provide our clients with independent, professional, and long-term service. Client wealth remains allocated at all times to the best regions, the best asset types, and the best fund managers at that moment — diversifying across single-region, single-asset, single-manager, and single-strategy risks so asset value can grow stably.

我们通过精选全球优秀的基金管理人和不同基金管理人的不同投资策略，为客户提供独立、专业和长期的服务，使客户的财富始终配置在当时时点下最好的投资区域、最好的资产种类和最好的基金管理人环境下，做到分散单个地域、单个资产、单个基金管理人和单个投资策略风险，为客户资产的稳健成长提供助力。

Underlying Assets底层资产

Fund products of excellent managers focused on the capital markets of China, the U.S., Australia, Europe, Japan, the Middle East, and emerging markets (India and South-East Asia) — after our due diligence and selection. 经过我们尽调与筛选的、专注于中国、美国、澳洲、欧洲、日本、中东和新兴市场国家（印度与东南亚）资本市场的优质基金管理人的基金产品。

Credit Services信贷服务

Mortgage and commercial-loan brokerage in Australia.澳洲房地产与商业贷款中介。

We provide loan-mortgage brokerage services for the Australian real-estate market, alongside other commercial-loan intermediation — a specialist capability within the group, held to the same standards of advice as our asset management practice.

为澳洲房地产市场提供房贷中介服务，并提供其他商业贷款中介——与资产管理业务同一标准的专业化能力。

Scope业务范围
